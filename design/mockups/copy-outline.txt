Assumptions
Built from the
tj-dashboard
repo, not the live render — I read Shell, Overview, Stats, Settings, Conversation and the measurement runbook.
Audience is station managers and HQ admins, so the home screen answers “is it running, and what needs me” before it shows analytics.
Station names, avatars and all figures are placeholders except the ones in the measurement runbook (2,209 contacted · 248 incremental · 3.96×).
Visual language follows the RapidScreen design system: Inter, flat surfaces, hairline rules, no shadows. The warm cream/Fraunces skin is gone — say the word if you want it kept.
What's repeated today
Bookings is printed three times on Stats
— the KPI card, the chart's “Bookings” stat, and the 50px number in the right aside. Same value, three weights.
Replied / No reply appears twice
— once above the weekly bars, once as “Reply split” in the aside. So does the due-soon / passed split, which is also the chart legend.
Four unlabelled “sent” numbers
— Total sent, Due soon sent, and three calendar-period cards, each on a different time window, none of them stated on the card.
Two answers to “how many bookings did outreach cause”
— Stats attributes by registration match, Value computes incremental lift. Neither page mentions the other.
Settings answers “is outreach on?” in two places
— auto-send toggles and station pause, each with its own explainer paragraph and its own coloured status banner.
The phone number renders three times
in a conversation — header title fallback, header subtitle, and the info panel's Phone row.
The move
One number, one place.
Each metric is rendered once at full size; everything else references it by name.
One period control
at the top of Performance, replacing per-panel windows.
Stats + Value become one page
with a method switch, so the two booking numbers sit side by side instead of on separate tabs.
Home becomes Today
— run state and a work queue, not a customer list.
Controls collapses to one matrix
: campaign × station, plus a master switch.
